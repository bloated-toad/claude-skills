# claude-skills
Some of my most useful Claude skills. Please enjoy!

## Skills

**[Forecast](forecast/)** — Transforms any developing situation into a structured probabilistic forecast. Researches from scratch, identifies key variables, generates named scenarios with explicit probabilities, and runs a calibration checkpoint where you assign your own estimate before comparing. The mandatory contrarian research and mid-conversation calibration mechanic train forecasting muscle over time.

**[Ingredient Riff](ingredient-riff/)** — Tell it what ingredients you have, get three opinionated options (practical, upgrade, and wildcard), then a complete recipe with actual measurements and sensory cues. Opinionated rather than encyclopedic, customizable to your actual kitchen setup, and focused on cooking right now rather than meal planning.

**[Prediction Tracker](prediction-tracker/)** — Maintains a persistent markdown prediction journal that captures vague hunches as scorable claims with explicit probabilities, resolution dates, and criteria. Tracks updates over time and reviews them with Brier scoring and calibration analysis. Lives where predictions happen (inside Claude conversations) and preserves the full reasoning trail so you can identify systematic biases.

**[Steelman](steelman/)** — Structured dialectical analysis: steelmans a claim, presents the best counter-case, then delivers a verdict that decomposes the claim into sub-claims with rough probabilities and identifies the key crux. Auto-detects debate format and, when analyzing back-and-forth arguments, shows both who won the debate as conducted and who is actually correct on the merits.

**[Thrift Store ID](thrift-store-id/)** — Identifies thrift store finds from 1–6 photos with a structured output covering ID, confidence level, fake warnings, current market pricing via web search, and a buy/pass verdict. Calibrated confidence tiers instead of false certainty, and verdicts that consider design quality and personal appeal alongside resale margins.

**[Wikipedia Editing Helper](wikipedia-analyzer/)** - Helps an aspiring Wikipedia editor locate opportunities to contribute and learn what makes good content work. Claude isn't editing, you are. This skill is meant to help overcome the initial hurdle and provide actionable advice. 

**[Wrap-Up](wrap-up/)** — End-of-conversation skill that generates a four-section deliverable: discussion narrative arc, distilled key insights, prompting critique, and bibliography. The prompting critique section provides specific critical feedback on what worked in your prompting and where Claude gave weak answers you didn't push back on.
